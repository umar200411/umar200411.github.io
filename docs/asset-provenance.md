## Research notes

- Beautify’s listing matched the exact application package and the developer name in the project brief. A different app also named Beautify was excluded.
- The Beautify appointment screenshot was not used because it visibly contained customer names and phone numbers.
- Zamindar Partner was verified against package `com.zamindartech.zamindarpartner`; the archive reports that this app was removed from Google Play in 2025. Its small product screenshot is used only as a clearly labeled company product reference, not as a claim about Umar's personal contribution.
- Token Talent screenshots came from the official ESS page’s image assets. They are presented as company product screens, not as proof that Umar personally built every screen.
- Magnet Masala’s current storefront supports a basket and sends order continuation to WhatsApp. That wording was checked against the live homepage/catalog and the actual basket drawer.
