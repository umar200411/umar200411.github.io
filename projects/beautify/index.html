Beautify — Making beauty services easier to discover and book.

An independently developed Android app that helps people find nearby salons, compare beauty services, and manage appointments, with salon-side tools for service and booking management.

FlutterDartFirebaseGoogle MapsGetX
View on Google Play ↗All projects ↗
OVERVIEW

One app for service seekers and salons.

Beautify brings salon discovery, service information, and appointment booking into a mobile experience. Customers can explore nearby providers and services; salons can set up a profile, publish service details, and manage bookings.

The product serves two connected audiences: people looking for beauty services and salon operators managing their offerings and appointments.

PRODUCT GOALS

Make the next step clear.

These are product goals reflected in the published feature set, not measured business outcomes.

01

Discover nearby salons

Use location-based discovery to help customers explore services around them.

02

Understand services

Give customers service descriptions and images before they choose an appointment.

03

Support booking follow-through

Help customers book appointments and keep track of them through confirmations, reminders, and calendar support.

04

Help salons manage listings

Let salon operators create a profile, organize services and prices, and manage bookings.

MY ROLE

Independent product development.

I independently developed and published Beautify. The portfolio’s project record identifies Flutter and Dart, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps and location services, GetX state management, and REST API integrations where applicable.

My work spans the mobile product and its connected workflows: navigation and state, authentication, service and booking experiences, location-aware discovery, and salon-side tools. The public Play listing describes Google sign-in, calendar support, appointment reminders, and salon service and booking management.
