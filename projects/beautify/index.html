Beautify Finding a salon and booking a time, in one app.

An Android app I built and published on my own. Customers use it to find salons nearby, compare services and book appointments. Salons use it to manage their services and bookings.

OVERVIEW

One app, two sides.

Customers open Beautify to find salons nearby, see what they offer and book a time. Salons set up a profile, list their services and prices, and keep track of their bookings.

GOALS

Make the next step clear.

What I wanted the app to get right.

01

Discover nearby salons

Use location-based discovery to help customers explore services around them.

02

Understand services

Give customers service descriptions and images before they choose an appointment.

03

Support booking follow-through

Help customers book appointments and keep track of them through confirmations, reminders, and calendar support.

04

Help salons manage listings

Let salon operators create a profile, organize services and prices, and manage bookings.

MY ROLE

I built it on my own.

I built and published Beautify on my own. It's made with Flutter and Dart, uses Firebase for sign-in, data and push notifications, Google Maps to find salons nearby, and GetX for state management.

That covered everything from navigation and sign-in to the booking flow, location-based search and the salon side of the app. It also has Google sign-in, appointment reminders and calendar support.
