02 / THE PROBLEM

Student life happens across too many tools.

Students often reach for separate applications to manage daily schedules, tasks and reminders, learning sessions, academic materials, expenses, habits, wellness activities, and prayer tracking.

Jumping between apps makes planning harder than it needs to be. Pulse puts all of it in one place.

  • Daily schedules, tasks, and reminders
  • Learning sessions and academic materials
  • Personal expenses, routines, and well-being
  • Mindful practices such as prayer tracking
03 / THE SOLUTION

One calm space to organize the day.

Pulse brings planning, classes, studying, money and daily routines into one app built around a student's day. The goal is less clutter and a clear idea of what to do next.

Simple navigationUnified experienceStudent-friendly flowsCareful UI/UXProgress at a glanceCalm visual language
04 / CORE APP FEATURES

A connected set of everyday tools.

Here's everything Pulse is meant to cover. It's still being built, so some of these are further along than others.

01

Today Dashboard

One screen for the day: tasks, routines, reminders and progress.

02

Planner, Tasks & Reminders

Daily, weekly, and monthly planning with checklists, deadlines, priorities, and reminders.

03

Learning & Focus

Learning goals, study sessions, focus timers, productivity tracking, and tools for managing distractions.

04

Academics

Organization for assignments, study materials, academic deadlines, and collaborative study workflows.

05

Academic Groups

Private groups for a teacher and their students. The teacher approves who joins and stays in charge.

Teacher flow

  • Create a group and generate a unique joining code.
  • Review and approve student join requests.
  • Manage members and assign student administrators.

Student flow

  • Enter a group code, name, and student ID.
  • Submit a request and access the group after approval.
  • Receive notifications for announcements and shared materials.

Teachers, and student admins they pick, can share announcements, documents, PDFs, images, videos and links. The teacher always has the final say.

06

Finance

Track income and expenses, set budgets and savings goals, and see where your money goes.

07

Wellness

Nutrition, exercise and general well-being, kept simple for students.

08

Namaz

Prayer times for your location, reminders, prayer tracking and Qibla direction.

09

Progress & Insights

Charts for study time, tasks, routines and streaks, so you can see how you're doing.

10

Live Weather

Current weather on your dashboard, later tied into the Living Sky visuals.

05 / SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE

Living Sky: a dashboard that moves with the time of day.

Living Sky is the idea behind the Pulse dashboard: a sky that follows the time of day, and the weather where it's available.

Think of a sun and moon that move across the screen, colours that shift from day to night, drifting clouds, stars and mountains in the distance. Weather effects are still on the to-do list.
