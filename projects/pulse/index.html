02 / THE PROBLEM

Student life happens across too many tools.

Students often reach for separate applications to manage daily schedules, tasks and reminders, learning sessions, academic materials, expenses, habits, wellness activities, and prayer tracking.

Moving between disconnected tools can make everyday planning feel more complicated than it needs to be. Pulse explores a simpler, more cohesive place to bring those routines together.

  • Daily schedules, tasks, and reminders
  • Learning sessions and academic materials
  • Personal expenses, routines, and well-being
  • Mindful practices such as prayer tracking
03 / THE SOLUTION

One calm space to organize the day.

Pulse brings productivity, academic organization, learning, finance, and personal routines into one student-first experience. Its product direction is to help people move through their day with less visual clutter and clearer next steps.

05PRODUCT SCOPE

Academic Groups

A private teacher–student collaboration concept with teacher approval and authority retained at the group level.

Teacher flow

  • Create a group and generate a unique joining code.
  • Review and approve student join requests.
  • Manage members and assign student administrators.

Student flow

  • Enter a group code, name, and student ID.
  • Submit a request and access the group after approval.
  • Receive notifications for announcements and shared materials.

Teachers and authorized student admins can share announcements, documents, PDFs, images, uploaded videos, educational links, and video links. Teachers retain final group authority.

05 / SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE

Pulse Living Sky — a dashboard that moves with time.

Living Sky is the concept for a real-time celestial and atmospheric visual experience: a dashboard environment that reflects time of day and, where supported, current weather.

The intended visual language includes sun and moon positioning, day-to-night color changes, animated clouds, stars and lunar details, layered atmosphere, mountain silhouettes, and gentle transitions. Weather-responsive appearance remains part of the evolving scope.
