Magnet Masala An online store for a Pakistani spice brand.

Shoppers browse the spices, choose a pack size, fill a basket and send the order over WhatsApp, on a phone or a laptop.

Visit Live Website ↗All projects ↗
OVERVIEW

A clear path from browsing to ordering.

The store sells recipe blends, everyday spices, quick seasonings and bundles. Customers browse the catalog, open a product, pick a pack size and add it to their basket.

Checkout happens on WhatsApp: the site writes the order message, and delivery details and confirmation are sorted out in the chat. There's no card payment on the site.

GOALS

Help shoppers find their flavour.

What the store needed to do well.

01

Make categories scannable

Group products across recipe blends, everyday spices, and quick seasonings.

02

Show useful product choices

Present product descriptions and pack-size options where available.

03

Support basket review

Let shoppers add products or curated sets and review basket contents.

04

Explain the order handoff

Make it clear that orders and delivery are confirmed on WhatsApp.

SHOPPING FLOW

Five steps to an order.

How a customer gets from the homepage to an order.

  1. 01

    Browse

    Explore products by category, search, or collection.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Open product details and select a listed pack size where available.

  3. 03

    Add

    Add individual products or curated bundles to the basket.

  4. 04

    Review

    Check selected items and quantities in the basket drawer.

  5. 05

    Continue

    Send the ready-made order message on WhatsApp and confirm the details.

MY PART
