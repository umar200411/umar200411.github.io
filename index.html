08 / FAQ

Before you
get in touch.

Quick answers to common questions. Anything else, just ask.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely, so where you're based doesn't matter. You can reach me by email or on WhatsApp, including my international number.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on the screens, features and backend your project needs. Send me a short brief and I'll get back to you with questions and an estimate.

How long will it take?

That depends on the scope too. Once I understand what you need, I'll give you a realistic timeline before any work starts.

Can you work on an app I already have?

Yes. I can fix bugs, update the design or add new features to an existing Flutter app.

Do you design the screens as well?

Yes. I can design the screens and build them, or build from designs you already have.

Can you help publish the app on Google Play?

Yes. I can prepare the release and help you publish it on Google Play, as I did with my own app, Beautify.
